# Pieces punish too much voice as much as too little

The game should be a test of skill, not of how loud or how long a player can shout. If Pieces only asked "did you jump far enough?", louder would always be safer and players would drift back to shouting. So Pieces also use low ceilings, overhangs and narrow landings, where a jump that is too loud bonks your head or overshoots the landing and drops you. The skill is hitting the right level. This works together with per-player Calibration and Sensitivity, where full Thrust is a comfortable strong voice, and with ADR 0007 (no chaser).
